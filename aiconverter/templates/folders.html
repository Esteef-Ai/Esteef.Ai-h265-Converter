H.264 + legacyAlso converts MPEG-1 (.mpg), VC-1 (.wmv) and DivX/Xvid (.avi/.mkv). These come out as .mkv (same name). WMV2/WMV3 are not included — tests showed they usually get bigger.
PriorityHigh-priority folders' files are processed before normal, then low. Changing it re-orders that folder's files that haven't started yet.
